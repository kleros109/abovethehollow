# Busy Executives Silently Divide Their Colleagues Into Two Groups:

![rw-book-cover](https://pbs.twimg.com/profile_images/1784227025940860929/a6t7PV2r.jpg)

- URL: https://twitter.com/moseskagan/status/1936411501281362326/?rw_tt_thread=False
## Summary
Busy executives categorize their colleagues into two groups. One group requires follow-up on tasks, while the other is trusted to complete tasks without reminders. To advance quickly, aim to be in the trustworthy group.

## Highlights
### id906121429


> Busy executives silently divide their colleagues into two groups:
> 1.  People to whom they give an instruction, then have to remember to follow up to make sure the instructions were followed
> 
> 2. People so trustworthy & effective that giving them instructions means the task can safely be assumed completed (unless some unexpected complexity arises)
