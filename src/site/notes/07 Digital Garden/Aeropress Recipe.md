 -  15g Coffee/250g water at 90 Celsius
- Grind coffee on medium-fine (25 on the Niche)
- Pour coffee into the Aeropress and shake to flatten   
- Start the *timer* and pour 100g in roughly 10 seconds
- At [1:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VXPKxowfXDQ&t=90s) pour the remaining 150g in roughly 15 seconds
- Stir with a spoon in back and forth or side to side motions 
- Place the piston to slow the dripping
- At [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VXPKxowfXDQ&t=150s) swirl and then begin pressing gently at a slow pace (30-45 seconds)
- Press through the hiss, finishing between [3:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VXPKxowfXDQ&t=180s)-[3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VXPKxowfXDQ&t=195s)
