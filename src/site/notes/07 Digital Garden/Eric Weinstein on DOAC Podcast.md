## Eric Weinstein's Closing Message Items

- Explore the songs of Tom Lehrer and Gilbert and Sullivan's operettas.
- Consider visiting the Azores and Indonesian archipelago, noting Indonesia's ease of language learning.
- Buy a poster of tropical fruits and visit each fruit's location before lights out.
- Listen to Bach's B minor mass and Pablo Casals' cello suites for inspiration.
- Watch Eva Cassidy's "Stormy Monday" for deep feelings, and James Carroll Booker's "The Resurrection of the Bayou Maharaja."
- Listen to Professor Longhairs "Big Chief"
- Visit the island of St. Helena in the South Atlantic.
- Check out Kurt Jai-Mungle's channel for unique content.
- Listen to Chris Buck’s version of "Miss You" by the Rolling Stones for groove.
- Explore great audio equipment from Neural DSP and Positive Grid.
- Get a good electric guitar, and experiment with open tuning and slides.
- Consider marriage and having children, emphasizing their importance.
- Dream big about legacy and leave a lasting mark.
- Avoid focus on narcissism and drama; seek meaning in simple pleasures like meteor showers.
- Recognize the importance of caring for others, especially in challenging times like the Middle East conflict.
- Be good to each other and try to find joy in life.
