Seth Godin suggests [two great questions for choosing a college](https://seths.blog/2023/12/on-choosing-a-college/):

> Are the people this place attracts the sort of people I want to spend time with and become more like?
> 
> Is the system that is in place here one that pushes and cajoles and processes people to become more like the kind of person I’d like to be?

If you’re looking to take your career to the next level—or in a new direction—these questions also work really well for choosing a company to work with.
