# The Simple Daily Planning Method That Top Performers Use

- Plan your day to be more organized and productive.
- Follow a daily planning sequence:
  1.  Check your calendar for appointments to see available time.
  2.  Look at your task manager and create a realistic task list based on the available time, removing unimportant tasks.
  3.  Prioritize the list, identifying the top two must-do tasks.
- Write down those top tasks on a physical A7 notebook to keep them in sight throughout the day.
