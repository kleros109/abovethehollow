"Develop into a lifelong self-learner through voracious reading; cultivate curiosity and strive to become a little wiser every day." 

 — Charlie Munger

This quote from Charlie Munger emphasizes the importance of continual learning and curiosity in personal development. Munger suggests that reading extensively and seeking knowledge can lead to lifelong growth and wisdom. This mindset encourages individuals to constantly seek new information and expand their understanding of the world.
