## The Power of Curiosity: A Paul Graham Reflection

> "Curiosity is the best guide. Your curiosity never lies, and it knows more than you do about what's worth paying attention to."
> — *How to Do Great Work*

In a world overflowing with distractions and prescribed paths, this insight is a powerful call to trust our own instincts and interests. Curiosity is not just a fleeting feeling—it's a compass that points us toward meaningful work, deeper learning, and unexpected discoveries. When we follow what genuinely intrigues us, we often find ourselves exploring new frontiers, developing unique perspectives, and creating value that can't be found by simply following the crowd. Graham's advice is a timely reminder to listen to that inner voice, let curiosity lead, and trust that it will take us somewhere worthwhile.
