# This Weekly Habit Changed My Business FOREVER

- Entrepreneurs struggle to make consistent progress due to lack of weekly planning.
- A 15-minute weekly planning session can transform a business.
- The session involves an audit (reviewing the past week) and a planning session (planning the coming week).
- The audit includes answering four questions: What went well? What didn't get done? What was not liked? What wins were achieved?
- The planning session involves: identifying the one thing to focus on, listing what's set in stone, and scheduling everything accordingly.
