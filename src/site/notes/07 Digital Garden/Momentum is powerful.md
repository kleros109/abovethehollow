>**How to break free from keeping it safe:**
>1. Identify one area where you feel most stuck.
> 2. Envision the boldest action that could break through that barrier.
> 3. Commit to one small step you can take right now to set that bold action in motion.
> 
> Whether it's making a call, sending an email, or setting up a meeting, do it now. Momentum begins with that first courageous step. 
> Progress is closer than you think.
