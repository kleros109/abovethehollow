Here is a prompt I created that was inspired by [[03 References/Matt Ragland\|Matt Ragland]]'s video, [[07 Digital Garden/If You Think Life Is Boring, Try This Notebook Trick\|If You Think Life Is Boring, Try This Notebook Trick]].

## Prompt
  
Review the following journal transcript(s). For each entry, identify and highlight any stories that meet these three criteria:

1. **Moment of Change:**

- Look for a specific event or realization where something shifted for the writer (e.g., expectations vs. reality, a turning point, or a new insight).

3. **Emotional Hook:**

- Note the emotional response or feelings associated with the moment of change (e.g., surprise, frustration, joy, relief).

5. **Lesson or Takeaway:**

- Summarize the main lesson, insight, or takeaway the writer gained from the experience.

 
**For each story you find, present your output in this format:**

- **Story Excerpt:** [Quote or paraphrase the relevant section]
- **Moment of Change:** [Describe the turning point]
- **Emotional Hook:** [Describe the emotion(s) involved]
- **Lesson/Takeaway:** [Summarize the insight or lesson]
  
If an entry does not contain all three elements, skip it.
  
**Example Output:**

- **Story Excerpt:** “I thought the meeting would be easy, but it turned out to be challenging.”
- **Moment of Change:** Realized the meeting was harder than expected.
- **Emotional Hook:** Felt unprepared and anxious.
- **Lesson/Takeaway:** Learned to prepare more thoroughly for future meetings.
