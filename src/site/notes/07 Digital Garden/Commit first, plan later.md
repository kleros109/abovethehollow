From [[Herbert Lui\|Herbert Lui]] [here.](https://herbertlui.net/commit-first-plan-later/)


<div class="transclusion internal-embed is-loaded"><div class="markdown-embed">



> “When you’re stuck, commit to action. Strategy will follow.”
>  
>In other words, if you’re making something new, planning too far ahead will likely just get you stuck.
> 
> You commit to a goal, then you use whatever resources you have to make it happen. Strategy emerges as you bump into the constraints.
> 
> The momentum that comes from committing and focusing on one challenge at a time—the one that’s in front of you—keeps you from worrying about the dozens of challenges that might happen.
