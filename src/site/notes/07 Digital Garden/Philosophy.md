Several notes in this vault link to [[07 Digital Garden/Philosophy\|Philosophy]], each offering a unique perspective on how philosophical ideas shape practical life. These notes cover themes from Stoic control to Japanese mindfulness, resilience, and the art of living in the present. Together, they form a web of insights that deepen and contextualize the core ideas in [[07 Digital Garden/Philosophy\|Philosophy]].

- **Identifying control in life choices**: Explores Epictetus’ core Stoic idea—distinguishing what’s within our control from what isn’t, and focusing our energy on the former.
- **How to earn your resilience**: Sahil Bloom (with a Marcus Aurelius quote) on building resilience through habits and values, especially in adversity.
- **Greg McKeown on Ichi-go Ichi-e**: Discusses the Japanese concept of cherishing each moment as unique, and the value of simplicity and presence.
- **Do not long for nor fear the next day**: Reflects on Seneca’s advice to live in the present, not worrying about the future, with highlights from “On the Shortness of Life.”
