Based on your Readwise highlights, here are some important health protocols and recommendations:

**1. Proactive Health Management & Monitoring:**
- Be proactive about health management through early detection and negotiation for better outcomes.
- Undergo regular scans for health conditions like aneurysms for timely intervention.
- Negotiate better prices for medical services by inquiring about discounts or lower costs.
- Focus on minimal lab testing, prioritizing critical markers like hemoglobin A1C and fasting lipids (triglycerides under 100) for metabolic health and insulin resistance.
- Monitor key health biomarkers: skeletal muscle mass, body fat, and regular blood pressure checks (especially after 30, taking home readings).
- Familiarize yourself with critical blood test markers, such as hemoglobin A1C and HSCRP (a measure of inflammation).
- Understand personal health data through genetic testing to tailor nutrition.

**2. Nutrition & Diet:**
- Optimize Omega-3 intake with fish oil supplements.
- Ensure adequate protein intake: at least 30 grams of high-quality dietary protein per meal to trigger muscle protein synthesis. Aim for 1 gram of protein per pound of ideal body weight daily, with 40-50 grams in the first meal.
- Start the day with a nutrient-packed smoothie after fasting overnight (consider adding whey protein, collagen powder, and moringa powder).
- Prioritize essential nutrients: 91 essential minerals (from mineral salts), omega-3 fatty acids (EPA and DHA from clean fish oil or plant-based options), and a complete amino acid supplement.
- Address specific nutrient deficiencies, e.g., B complex vitamins for anxiety.
- Cut out ultra-processed foods, sodas, and seed oils.
- Limit sugars and processed carbohydrates.

**3. Exercise & Movement:**
- Engage in resistance training to build muscle and strength.
- Incorporate morning exercise for deeper, more restorative sleep and improved overall sleep efficiency.
- "Move Your Body" as one of the four elements of health.

**4. Fasting & Detox:**
- Consider intermittent fasting once a week (e.g., a 24-hour reset diet).
- Perform a 14 or 21-day functional medicine detox once a quarter (every 12 weeks).
- Fasting is best in the evening, not in the morning (stop eating 3-4 hours before bed).

**5. Heat & Cold Exposure:**
- Engage in cold exposure (e.g., ice baths for 3-6 minutes at 10 degrees Celsius, or cold showers) for a total of 11 minutes each week.
- Utilize sauna usage (e.g., 163-degree Fahrenheit sauna for 30 minutes) for heat stress benefits, totaling 57 minutes spread over three or more days.
- Heat stress can also be induced by being in 104-degree Fahrenheit water up to the shoulders for 20 minutes.

**6. Mental & Emotional Well-being:**
- Prioritize sleep and rest.
- Incorporate breathwork techniques (e.g., Wim Hof method).
- "Love Your People & Let Yourself Be Loved" as one of the four elements of health.
- To translate belief into action for happiness, understand, practice, and share specific habits and practices.

**7. Other:**
- Maintain adequate Vitamin D levels.
- Increased intracellular zinc concentrations efficiently impair replication/reproduction of a number of viruses.
