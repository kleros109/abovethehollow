# How to plan an epic 2025 in 56 minutes | Jesse Itzler

1. **Review and Reflect:** Close out the year by reviewing personal achievements and areas for improvement. Write handwritten thank-you letters to those who impacted you.
2. **Identify B Minuses:** Assess different life areas (finances, relationships, etc.) to pinpoint aspects that need improvement.
3. **Set a Misogi:** Choose one significant challenge or goal for the year that will define your experience.
4. **Mini Adventures:** Plan a mini-adventure every eight weeks to break routine and create memorable experiences.
5. **Add Winning Habits:** Introduce one new positive habit each quarter to enhance personal growth.
6. **Visual Planning:** Use a large calendar to visualize and map out key events and adventures for the year.
