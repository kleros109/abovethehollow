> **Minimize Overhead for Maximum Focus**
> 
> **To enhance productivity, one must address and minimize 'overhead tax,' which represents the excessive mental load caused by constant interruptions and unproductive tasks.** 
> 
> It's crucial to consolidate workloads to stay below a critical threshold that allows for focused work. **Fragmented schedules hinder progress; therefore, protecting dedicated time for important tasks is essential.** Implementing strategies like post-meeting processing can help clean up time. 
> 
> Furthermore, avoid ad hoc, unscheduled messaging, which disrupts collaboration and concentration. Opt for structured communication methods, such as office hours and process-driven emails, to facilitate effective teamwork.
