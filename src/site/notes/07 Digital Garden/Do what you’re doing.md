The phrase "Age Quod Agis," meaning "Do what you are doing," encourages us to focus fully on the task at hand. In a world filled with distractions, being intentional and present can lead to greater productivity and connection. Embracing this mindset can help us achieve more by reducing the cognitive cost of multitasking.
