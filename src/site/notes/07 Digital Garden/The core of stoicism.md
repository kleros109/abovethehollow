> Two points in the Stoic system deserve special mention. 
> 
> One is a __**careful distinction between things which are in our power and things which are not.**__ Desire and dislike, opinion and affection, are within the power of the will; whereas health, wealth, honour, and other such are generally not so. 
> 
> __**The Stoic was called upon to control his desires and affections, and to guide his opinion; to bring his whole being under the sway of the will or leading principle,**__ just as the universe is guided and governed by divine Providence.
