## Core Components

*   **Calendar, Task Manager, Notes App**: Use a combination of a calendar app (e.g., Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar), a task manager (e.g., TickTick, Todoist, Microsoft To Do, Apple Reminders), and a notes app (e.g., Mem, Notion, Obsidian, RemNote, Amplenote, Google Drive).  See [[07 Digital Garden/Productivity System\|Productivity System]].
*   **CODE (Capture, Organize, Distill, Express)**:  A system for processing information. See [[07 Digital Garden/Productivity System\|Productivity System]].
*   **Core Systems (Cal Newport)**:  Incorporate daily/weekly planning, GTD task capture, deep work rituals, exercise, and systems for ideas. See [[07 Digital Garden/Core Systems\|Core Systems]].

## Task Management & Prioritization

*   **Ivy Lee Method**: At the end of each day, write down the six most important things to do tomorrow, prioritize them, and focus on the first one until it's done. Carry over unfinished tasks. See [[04 Clippings/The Ultimate Productivity System\|04 Clippings/The Ultimate Productivity System]].
*   **2+8 Prioritization**: Reduce tasks to the most critical ones.
*   **3x5 Notecard**: Each evening, write down 3-5 high-impact to-dos for the next day. Use the back for minor tasks. See [[07 Digital Garden/Here's a simple to-do list trick anyone can use\|Here's a simple to-do list trick anyone can use]].
*   **Action Method**: Break down everything into projects with Action Steps, References, and Backburner Items. See [[07 Digital Garden/The Action Method\|The Action Method]].

## Time Management & Focus

*   **Time Blocking**: Schedule blocks of time for specific activities. See [[04 Clippings/Readwise/Articles/Carl Pullein - Time and Priorities\|04 Clippings/Readwise/Articles/Carl Pullein - Time and Priorities.md]].
*   **"Opt-Out" Approach**: When identifying a high-priority task, schedule it for a specific time. See [[07 Digital Garden/4 Steps to Hyperfocus Productivity System\|4 Steps to Hyperfocus Productivity System]].
*   **Protected Time**: Keep mornings free from calls for uninterrupted work.
*   **Weekly Templates (Cal Newport)**: Use weekly templates with protected time, daily themes, rules, and autopilot scheduling. See [[07 Digital Garden/The Weekly Productivity Template To Achieve More\|The Weekly Productivity Template To Achieve More]], [[02 Notes/Weekly Template\|02 Notes/Weekly Template]].
*   **Slow Productivity (Cal Newport)**: Do fewer things, work at a natural pace, obsess over quality. See [[04 Clippings/Readwise/Books/Cal Newport - Slow Productivity\|04 Clippings/Readwise/Books/Cal Newport - Slow Productivity.md]].

## Key Principles

*   **Important vs. Urgent**: Focus on important, not urgent, tasks. See [[04 Clippings/Readwise/Articles/Sahil Bloom's Curiosity Chronicle - The Ultimate Productivity Tool\|04 Clippings/Readwise/Articles/Sahil Bloom's Curiosity Chronicle - The Ultimate Productivity Tool.md]].
*   **Eliminate Distractions**: Remove things that prevent you from achieving goals. See [[07 Digital Garden/4 Steps to Hyperfocus Productivity System\|4 Steps to Hyperfocus Productivity System]].
*   **Ruthless with Time**: Protect your time by declining unnecessary meetings.
*   **"Hell Yeah or No"**: Say no to activities that don't excite you. See [[04 Clippings/Readwise/Articles/Ali Abdaal - I've read pretty much every book on productivity and time...\|04 Clippings/Readwise/Articles/Ali Abdaal - I've read pretty much every book on productivity and time....md]].
*   **Minimize Overhead**: Reduce mental load from interruptions. See [[04 Clippings/Readwise/Podcasts/Deep Questions with Cal Newport - Ep. 319 — Reclaim Your Time.md\|04 Clippings/Readwise/Podcasts/Deep Questions with Cal Newport - Ep. 319 — Reclaim Your Time.md]].
