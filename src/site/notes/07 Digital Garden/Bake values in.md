> **Bake Values In**
> - Live your values from the start, like adding sugar to a cake mix.
> - Don't wait for "one day" to live your values; integrate them now.
> - Ironically, you're more productive when you start with your values as constraints.
> - Remove bottlenecks by clarifying what you really want from the beginning.
> - This applies to life and work, such as founders building companies.
