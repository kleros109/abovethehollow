# How I Manage My Time - The Weekly Productivity Template To Achieve More | Cal Newport

![How I Manage My Time - The Weekly Productivity Template To Achieve More | Cal Newport](https://www.youtube.com/embed/JRRk3VZOVpk&t=2272s)

# ONE SENTENCE SUMMARY:
Cal Newport advocates using weekly templates with protected time, daily themes, rules, and autopilot scheduling to enhance productivity, adjusting them quarterly.

# MAIN POINTS:
1. Weekly templates bridge the gap between quarterly and weekly plans.
2. Protected time involves dedicating specific time blocks for certain activities.
3. Exception handling routines compensate for interruptions to protected time.
4. Daily themes assign different types of activities to specific days.
5. Regular rules and limits enforce boundaries on incoming tasks.
6. Autopilot scheduling predetermines when regular tasks will occur.
7. Templates reduce the need to rethink weekly planning elements.
8. Quarterly reviews help adjust templates based on effectiveness.
9. Grappling with ideal weeks provides insights into work life.
10. Weekly templates improve productivity and reduce frustration.

# ACTION ITEMS:
1. Create a weekly template for the current quarter, including protected time, daily themes, rules, and autopilot scheduling.
2. Review and adjust the weekly template at the end of the quarter based on what worked and what didn't.
