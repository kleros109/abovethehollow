## Summary
- **4 steps to hyperfocus**
	- **Subtract** things that prevent you from achieving goals and target - the crap
	- **Add** the systems to achieve goal
		- **Clarity** on what it is that you need to do - clear goals and targets
		- **Prioritize** the action that will help you solve the solve the problem getting in way of  achieving the goal
		- **Time management** using an "opt-out" approach
			- Once you identify a high priority task, what is preventing me from doing it now? can I do it this afternoon, tonight, or tomorrow? Set an implementation intention by scheduling it for a specific time.
		- **Execution** only comes after you've solved for clarity, priority, and time problems
			- Execution is about self-talk - what is the internal resistance to getting things done?
			- What is creating resistance or fear which limits getting starting on the tasks
	- **Divide** your day to focus on different aspects of the system 
	- **Multiply** your efforts to become more productive
		- Everyday do the same thing, be consistent - inspired by [[04 Clippings/Readwise/Books/James Clear - Atomic Habits\|James Clear - Atomic Habits]]
- **Steps to the system**
	- What is the relevant lens? - Work, Personal, All, etc
	- What is the goal or target?
	- Identify the bottleneck preventing you from achieving goal
	- What are the steps to solve the bottleneck
