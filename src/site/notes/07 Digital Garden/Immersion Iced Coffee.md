## Master Recipe

- 500ml total water
- 330ml hot water
- 170ml ice
- 37.5g coffee
- Steep for 5 minutes
- 2-3 drops saline solution (80% water, 20% coffee)
- 13.3:1 total water:coffee
- 2/3 brew water, 1/3 ice
## Aeropress Recipe

- 20g coffee
- 179g hot water
- 88g ice (5 cubes)
- Steep for 5 minutes
