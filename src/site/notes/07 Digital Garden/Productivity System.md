## Overview

Create a plan to improve productivity at school and work through the use of 3 core applications:

- **Calendar** - Google, Outlook or Apple
- **Task Manager** - Tick Tick, Todoist, Microsoft To Do, Apple Reminders
- **Notes/Reference App** - Mem, Notion, Obsidian, RemNote, Amplenote, Google Drive

## Action Steps

- Choose an app to manage your calendar - Apple, Google, or Outlook
- Choose a task manager to create to do lists and manage projects
- Choose a notes or storage app to distill/express notes and save references for future use
- Develop a system to CODE (capture, organize, distill, and express)
