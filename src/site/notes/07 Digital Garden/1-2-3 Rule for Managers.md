> Picking what task to do first can often take just as long as actually completing one.
> And as managers, we just don’t have that time to waste.
> 
> Enter the **1-2-3 Rule**: A streamlined way to structure your day in just 3 minutes.
>
> Here's how it works:
> 
> **1. Deep Work on Your Top Priority (3 hours)**
> • Define what success looks like at the end of this block 
> • Break it into smaller chunks to ensure steady progress 
> • Take a mid-session break, but don’t open Slack or email
> 
> **2. Break the Bottleneck Meetings (1 hour each)**
> • Schedule these later in the day during collaboration windows 
> • One meeting focuses on unblocking one of your priorities 
> • The other helps a team member move their work forward
> 
> **3. Urgent Clear-Out Sprint (1 hour)**
> • Spend 30 minutes knocking out pressing tasks 
> • Use the next 30 to diagnose why it’s urgent and fix it
> 
> **Why it works:**
> • You’re forced to prioritize the truly important work
> • You get a balance of strategic and tactical tasks
> • You help prevent the next wave of urgent fires
> 
> **Pro tip:** Use this method with your team. Have each member share their 1-2-3 plan.
> It's a super quick way to align priorities and spot potential bottlenecks.
> 
> **Remember: the goal is never perfection. It's progress.**
