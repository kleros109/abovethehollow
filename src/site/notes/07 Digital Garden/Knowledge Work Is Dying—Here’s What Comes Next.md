## Summary
AI is taking over knowledge-based jobs, making emotional clarity, discernment, and connection essential skills for the future. As expertise becomes less valuable, wisdom work will become more important in the workplace. To thrive in this new age, individuals must focus on developing their emotional intelligence and building genuine human connections.
